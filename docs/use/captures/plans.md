Screenshots and screen recording come with every Hippius account that has Drive storage: the free 10 GB, or a paid plan. The Free plan has a few limits on recordings, and paid plans lift them.

If you signed in with a 12-word access key instead of Google, GitHub or Apple, your account doesn't come with the free 10 GB, so you'll need a plan before you can capture.

## Free and paid, side by side

| | Free plan | Paid plans (Starter, Plus, Max, Scale) |
|---|---|---|
| **Screenshots** | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Sharing** | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Length of a recording** | Up to 5 minutes | No limit |
| **Number of recordings** | 25 in total | No limit |
| **Hippius watermark** | A small one in the corner | None |
| **Insights, comments and reactions** | See how many people opened a link | Full Insights, comments and reactions, on for captures |
| **Storage** | Captures count toward your 10 GB | Captures count toward your plan's storage |

The only limit on a paid plan is your storage.

## Recordings stop at 5 minutes

On the Free plan, a recording stops on its own when it reaches 5 minutes, but you won't be caught out. In the last minute the timer counts down, and when time's up the recording is saved, uploaded and shared exactly like one you stopped yourself, so nothing is lost. Paused time doesn't count, so a pause to gather your thoughts doesn't eat into your 5 minutes.

The card after the recording has an Upgrade button if you need longer.

## 25 recordings

The Free plan includes 25 recordings in total. The Captures page shows how many you've used, for example **3 of 25 free recordings used**, and the capture bar shows it too, in the desktop app and in the console.

Delete a recording and you get the slot back straight away. Moving it out of your captures drive does the same. Screenshots never count.

Every video in your captures drive counts, whatever it's called and whichever folder it's in, and whether you recorded it in the desktop app, in the console or on another computer. That includes a captures folder you picked yourself, or one inside another drive, and moving your captures folder to another place doesn't start the count again.

When you've used all 25, Hippius tells you as soon as you press Record, before anything is recorded, so you never lose a take. Upgrade your plan to record more, or delete an older recording to make room.

## The watermark

Screenshots and recordings made on the Free plan carry a small Hippius watermark (the Hippius logo and the word "Hippius") in the bottom-right corner. It's white and slightly see-through, so it reads on light and dark backgrounds without getting in the way. Your camera bubble sits in the opposite corner.

The watermark is drawn into the picture on your computer, before the capture is encrypted. It isn't something the server adds or could remove, so it stays on the file when you share it or download it. Captures made on a paid plan have no watermark. Upgrading doesn't remove it from captures you've already taken.

## Insights, comments and reactions

On a paid Drive plan, links to your screenshots and recordings come with full [Insights](/use/captures/insights): views, viewers, how much of a recording each person watched and where they stopped, plus comments and reactions, all switched on for you.

On the Free plan, Insights show how many times a link was opened and by how many people; completion, the chart of where people stopped and the viewer list come with paid plans. New links on the Free plan start with Insights, comments and reactions off, in the desktop app and the console, and turning one on shows an upgrade prompt. Links that already had one on keep it until you turn it off. See [Where the switches start](/use/captures/insights#where-the-switches-start).

## Storage

A capture is a file, so it counts toward your storage like any other: 10 GB on the Free plan, or your paid plan's size. Its share link keeps a separate encrypted copy, and that copy counts too, so a shared capture uses about twice its size until you turn the link off (see [Share links for captures](/use/captures/share-links#storage)).

To see how much room you have, or to change plan, see [Billing in the console](/use/console/billing#drive-plans) or [in the desktop app](/use/desktop/billing).
