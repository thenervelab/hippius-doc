The questions people ask most often about the Hippius extension, with short answers and a link to the full story.

### Do I need an account?

To save and share, yes: every capture is kept in your own Hippius Drive. To try it first, no. <strong>Capture without saving</strong> in the popup takes screenshots and recordings straight away, and they wait on your computer until you connect. [Why you need a Hippius account](/use/browser-extension/account#why-you-need-a-hippius-account).

### Is it free?

Yes. The Free plan includes unlimited screenshots and share links, 25 recordings of up to 5 minutes, and 10 GB of storage, with a small Hippius watermark on each capture. Paid plans lift the recording limits, remove the watermark and add insights and comments. [Plans and Limits](/use/browser-extension/plans).

### What's the console?

The Hippius console, at [console.hippius.com](https://console.hippius.com), is the website where your account and your files live. You sign in there, set your unlock password, and connect the extension. Your captures show up there too, under **Captures**.

### Which password does it ask for?

Your Hippius unlock password, not your Google, GitHub or Apple password. It's a password you create once in the console, and it protects the keys to your files. A brand-new account doesn't have one yet, so set it in the console's Settings before you connect. [Set your unlock password](/use/browser-extension/get-started#set-your-unlock-password).

### I forgot my unlock password. What now?

Use the 12 words the console showed you when you set it. Click <strong>Forgot your password?</strong> in the password dialog, enter the 12 words, and choose a new password. Nobody else can reset it, us included. [If you forget your password](/use/console/unlock-password#if-you-forget-your-password).

### Who can see my recordings?

The people you send the link to, and anyone they pass it on to. The key that opens a capture is inside its link, and it never reaches us, so we can't watch your recordings. Without the link, nobody can open them. [Privacy and Permissions](/use/browser-extension/privacy).

### Do links expire?

No. A link keeps working until you turn it off in the console. If you want one that expires, or one with a password, make a second link in the console. [Who can open a link, and for how long](/use/browser-extension/sharing#who-can-open-a-link-and-for-how-long).

### Why does it ask me to connect again after I restart my browser?

The connection to your account is kept only while your browser is open, so nothing that can save to your account is left on your computer. After a restart, click Connect account and Connect again. It takes a few seconds. [Staying connected](/use/browser-extension/account#staying-connected).

### Is my face in a window recording?

Yes, from version 0.3.0. With Screen and the camera on, your camera is drawn into the corner of a window or tab recording, and shown where the floating window is when you share your entire screen. The floating controls stay out of the video either way. [Your camera with Screen](/use/browser-extension/recording#your-camera-with-screen).

### Can I use it in Firefox or Safari?

Not yet. The extension is for Chrome and Microsoft Edge today. In Safari or Firefox, you can take screenshots and record from the [Hippius console](/use/captures) in your browser, with nothing to install.
