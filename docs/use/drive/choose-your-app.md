Drive is one account and one set of files in three apps. Sign in to any of them with the same account, enter your unlock password, and your files are there. Most people use more than one.

| | Console | Desktop App | Mobile App |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Runs on** | Any web browser | Windows, macOS and Linux | Android (iPhone coming soon) |
| **Get it** | [console.hippius.com](https://console.hippius.com) | [Download](/use/desktop/getting-started#installing-the-desktop-app) | [Google Play](/use/mobile/getting-started#install-android) |
| **Best for** | Getting to your files from any computer | Keeping folders on your computer in sync | Your phone's photos and videos |

## Console

Use the console when you want your files without installing anything. Upload files of up to 100 MB each, open them in the browser, download a whole folder as a zip, and share links.

  <li><a href="/use/console/drive">Drive</a>: browse, upload, preview and download.</li>
  <li><a href="/use/console/unlock-password">Unlock Password</a>: set it up, and restore access with your recovery seed.</li>
  <li><a href="/use/console/shared-links">Shared Links</a>: share files and folders, and manage your links.</li>
  <li><a href="/use/console/billing#drive-plans">Billing</a>: choose, change or cancel your Drive plan.</li>

  <li><a href="/use/desktop/shared-links">Shared Links</a> and <a href="/use/desktop/share-from-finder">Share from Finder</a>: share files and folders.</li>

## One unlock password in every app {#unlock-password}

You set your unlock password once, and the same one then opens your files in every app. Each app asks for it at a different moment:

| App | Asks you to set it | Asks for it again |
|---|---|---|
| **Console** | The first time you upload a file or create a folder | The first time you upload, preview or download in each browser session |
| **Desktop App** | Right after your first sign-in | When you sign in on a new computer |
| **Mobile App** | Before your first upload on a new account | Once on a new phone, after you sign in |

If you forget it, your 12-word recovery seed restores access. The [Console](/use/console/unlock-password#if-you-forget-your-password) and [Mobile App](/use/mobile/settings#restore-access) guides walk you through it.

## One plan for every app

Your Drive plan covers your whole account. Everything you upload from any app counts toward the same storage. You can subscribe, change or cancel a plan in the [console](/use/console/billing#drive-plans) or the [desktop app](/use/desktop/billing). The mobile app shows your plan but can't change it.
